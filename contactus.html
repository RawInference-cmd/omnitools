🛡️ 100% Client-Side Privacy FAQ

Do my files ever leave my browser?

Never. Every PDF merge, image compression, spreadsheet conversion, and calculation executes locally in your browser memory. Zero files are uploaded or stored on any server.

Can support recover a document I processed?

No. Because we never receive or retain your documents, only your device holds the processed output. Be sure to download your converted files before closing the tab.

What should I include in a bug report?

Share the tool name, your browser version, and approximate file type or size (never send confidential files). You can review our full guarantees on the Privacy Policy page.
