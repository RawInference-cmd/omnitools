Flappy Bird Arcade

Navigate your bird through green pipes using quick reflexes and rhythm.

🎮 WebGL Powered

🕹️ How to Play

🖱️
Mouse Click / Tap Click or tap anywhere on the game screen to flap your wings upward.
⌨️
Spacebar Key Press the Space key on desktop keyboards to maintain steady flight altitude.
🎯
Game Objective Pass cleanly between as many pipes as possible without touching obstacles or the ground.

💡 Pro Tip for High Scores

Tap in short, calculated bursts rather than holding down. Gravity accelerates quickly, so steady timing is key.
