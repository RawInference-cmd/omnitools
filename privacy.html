📜 Detailed Privacy Policy

1. Client-Side Architecture & Zero Uploads

OmniTools is built as a pure client-side web application. When you select a PDF, image, spreadsheet, or text snippet for processing, all parsing, rendering, compression, and conversion happen locally inside your browser's memory. OmniTools operates no file-processing backend and never receives copies of your files.

2. Information We Do Not Collect

We do not require account registration, and we do not collect names, passwords, file metadata, document contents, financial inputs, or personal identifiers while you use our tools. If you contact us voluntarily via email, your message is used solely to respond to your support request.

3. Browser LocalStorage & Preferences

OmniTools uses your browser's localStorage solely to enhance your local experience—such as remembering your Dark/Light theme (theme), pinned favorites (omnitools_favorites), recently opened tools (omnitools_recent), and locally saved notes in the Notes App. This data never leaves your browser and can be cleared anytime via browser settings.

4. External Libraries & Network Requests

Certain specialized tools load open-source client-side libraries (such as PDF.js, pdf-lib, jsPDF, JSZip, or SheetJS) from trusted CDNs. Only tools that explicitly require live web data—such as the Currency Converter or URL-based SEO analyzers—make outbound requests for the specific URL or currency pair you enter.

5. Policy Updates & Contact

We may update this Privacy Policy as new client-side tools are added to OmniTools. For questions or compliance inquiries, email support@omnitools.app.
